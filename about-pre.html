The world changed.
The curriculum didn't.

Our Origin

Born from the gap every African deserves to close.

Across Africa and around the world, millions of people — students, graduates, professionals, and entrepreneurs — carry qualifications but lack the practical intelligence to navigate careers, build businesses, and lead themselves and others effectively.

The problem was not intelligence. It was curriculum. Nobody had built a structured institution to teach the intelligence that life actually demands — until now.

Life Beyond Class is not a response to what was missing in one person's education. It is a response to a generational gap that has held back the potential of millions — and a bold commitment to close it.
